# WEALTHY WHALES — REGULATORY COMPLIANCE & LEGAL GUARDRAILS (`docs/COMPLIANCE.md`)

## 1. Regulatory Status & SEBI Disclaimers

Wealthy Whales is an advanced technology platform providing algorithmic market analytics, quantitative models, and educational tools for Indian capital markets (NSE & BSE).

**Crucial Legal Guardrail**:
- Wealthy Whales is **NOT** a SEBI-registered Investment Adviser (IA) or Research Analyst (RA).
- Under SEBI (Investment Advisers) Regulations, 2013 and SEBI (Research Analysts) Regulations, 2014, offering personalized investment advice or buy/sell recommendations for a fee requires formal registration.
- Wealthy Whales strictly provides **general quantitative screening, mathematical pattern recognition, statistical backtests, and educational analytics**.
- No output from Wealthy Whales constitutes financial advice, investment advice, trading advice, or a recommendation to buy, sell, or hold any security, derivative, or financial product.

## 3. Mandatory Copy & Terminology Guidelines
- **Permitted Terms**: `Signal`, `Setup`, `Quantitative Bias`, `Mathematical Confluence`, `Study Idea`, `Historical Regularity`.
- **Prohibited Terms**: `Recommendation`, `Stock Tip`, `Guaranteed Return`, `Sure-Shot Profit`, `Call/Put Advisory`.

## 4. Fair Use & Third-Party Data Ethics
- All public exchange data and corporate filings are scraped politely with rate-limiting, randomized user-agents, and exponential backoff to minimize server load.
- No copyrighted third-party proprietary data is resold.
- Creator courses carry explicit individual creator disclosures that creators are independent educators and do not guarantee profits.
